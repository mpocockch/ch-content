# Sources: "What Electrical Testing Do Insurance Companies Expect in Commercial Facilities?"

Primary reference: **FM Global Property Loss Prevention Data Sheet 5-20, Electrical Testing**
(October 2016, interim revision October 2025, 21 pages). Tier 1 for what FM Global recommends
(it is the insurer's own document). Supplied by Matt as the PDF attached to the Asana card
(`FMDS0520 3.pdf`) and read in full in the 2026-10-22 run. The PDF is copyrighted by Factory
Mutual Insurance Company and states no part may be reproduced, so it is not committed to this
repo; the body paraphrases and cites section content only. Public source: fm.com (FM Property
Loss Prevention Data Sheets).

## Claims in the body (all from DS 5-20 unless noted)

- Scope: low-voltage equipment incl. supply/distribution transformers 5 MVA and below; excludes
  low-power data/comm/process controls (s1.0). Low voltage means 600V or less per IEEE 141 (App. A).
- Majority of losses tied to operating conditions or environment; preventable by visual
  inspection, housekeeping, IR and insulation-resistance tests (s1.1).
- Visual inspections monthly/quarterly; grounding conductors every 1 to 3 years (s2.1.1).
- Frequencies are a general guide to adjust for equipment, duty, failure history, criticality
  and condition (s2.1.3).
- IR survey annually; Oct 2025 table of actions by temperature difference, for use when no
  manufacturer guidance or standard exists (s2.1.3.1, Table 2.1.3.1.1, App. B).
- LV breakers every 3 to 5 years, critical/main priority; test table incl. 50% resistance
  spread, 100 Mohm insulation, primary current injection; direct-trip breakers need primary
  injection (s2.1.3.2, Table 2.1.3.2.1).
- Fuses: 15% phase deviation; replace all three phases (s2.1.3.4). ATS every 3 to 5 years,
  manufacturer lubricant only (s2.1.3.5).
- Fluid-filled transformers: fluid screen and DGA at least every 1 to 2 years; PCB check every
  5 years, 50 ppm; electrical tests every 3 to 5 years; 100 Mohm LV / 5000 Mohm HV; power
  factor under 3% (s2.1.3.7, Table 2.1.3.7.3). Dry-type: every 3 to 5 years, PI not under 1.0
  (s2.1.3.8).
- Emergency generators: monthly start, diesel 30 min at 30% nameplate or manufacturer minimum;
  wet stacking explanation; 5 Mohm at 40C, DAR not under 1.4 (s2.1.3.9). Induction motors
  monthly visual, tests every 3 to 5 years where no contingency plan (s2.1.3.12).
- Ground resistance not to exceed 1 ohm; test triggers (s2.1.3.13).
- Studies required and repeat triggers, 50 hp / 37 kW (s2.2).
- Switchroom temperature and the 18F (10C) insulation-life halving rule; clean/cool/dry/tight
  lists; 10-minute walk-through; 5 ft combustible clearance (s3.6).
- Ultrasonic scanning; DGA trending over absolute values; IR view ports (s3.4.2, 3.5, 3.7).
- Reference list names NFPA 70 (NEC), IEEE standards and NETA MTS, not NFPA 70B (s4.2). Checked
  directly against the reference list.
