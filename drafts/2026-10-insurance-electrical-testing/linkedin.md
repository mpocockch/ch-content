Your insurer has an opinion about your electrical room.

FM Global's Data Sheet 5-20 spells out what that opinion looks like for low-voltage equipment, and it goes well beyond an annual scan.

Most of it is simple. Most of it is also undocumented.

What the sheet covers:
→ Monthly or quarterly walk-throughs for clean, cool, dry and tight conditions
→ A switchroom held at or below 86°F
→ Breaker, switch and transfer switch testing every 3 to 5 years
→ Oil screening and dissolved gas analysis on fluid-filled transformers every 1 to 2 years
→ A monthly loaded run for emergency generators

The October 2025 revision also added a table for ranking infrared findings, so a hot connection gets a repair window instead of a debate.

These are recommendations, not your policy. But the engineer who walks your site will measure you against them.
