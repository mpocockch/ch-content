# Sources: "How Do NFPA 70E, NFPA 70B and the OSHA General Duty Clause Work Together?"

nfpa.org, ecmweb.com and osha.gov-adjacent trade domains are blocked by the egress proxy
(WebFetch returned EGRESS_BLOCKED for ecmweb.com). All claims below come from WebSearch
synthesis, one step removed from primary text. No NFPA text is quoted verbatim anywhere in
the body, and no section numbers are cited.

## Claims in the body

- **General Duty Clause text (Section 5(a)(1)): workplace free from recognized hazards causing or
  likely to cause death or serious physical harm.** Tier 1 (statute), via search synthesis.
- **OSHA does not enforce NFPA 70E directly but consensus standards can be evidence of hazard
  recognition and feasible abatement.** Tier 3 (ECM, "The Importance of NFPA 70E to OSHA
  Violations and Fines"; "How OSHA Uses NFPA 70E"), plus OSHA standard interpretation letters
  surfaced in search (osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations). Not read directly.
- **70E 2024 added Informative Annex S, Assessing the Condition of Maintenance; the next edition
  adds an informational note under the condition-of-maintenance provision pointing to Annex S,
  which references NFPA 70B.** Tier 3 (graceport.com vendor blog, weakest tier) corroborated in
  topic by ECM "Understanding NFPA 70E and the Condition of Maintenance" (title and snippet only).
  Body describes it in general terms and hedges with "secondary sources describe".
- **NFPA 70B became a full standard with the 2023 edition ("shall" language).** General industry
  knowledge, consistent with the published Breaker Testing exemplar on this blog. Not re-verified
  against NFPA.
- **Arc flash studies rely on protective device behavior.** General engineering knowledge.

## Left out of the body

- Any 70E section numbers (110.3(C), Annex S sub-clause S.8): secondary or vendor sources only.
- Any exact issuance date for the next 70E edition. Search results conflict ("in effect" vs
  "will be out in 2027"); body says "next edition" and tells the reader to confirm with NFPA.
- Verbatim quotation of 70E or 70B. See Asana reply to the reviewer's question.
- The card's claim that the new edition "adds language connecting maintenance documentation to
  70E risk assessments": stronger than the sources support, so the body uses the milder
  Annex S pointer framing.
