# Hero image brief

## Generation prompt

A wide 16:9 documentary photograph of an experienced electrician in correct arc-flash personal
protective equipment — full arc-rated flash suit jacket, hood with a clear face shield fully
covering the neck and face, heavy voltage-rated rubber gloves under leather protectors —
standing in front of an open industrial panelboard in a Connecticut manufacturing facility,
holding a printed circuit directory sheet up against the inside of the open panel door to
compare it to the breakers, cool fluorescent overhead lighting, shallow depth of field with
switchgear receding into the background, no readable text or signage anywhere in frame.

## Composition notes

- Subject and panel positioned left-of-center or centered, leaving clear space in the upper
  right third for a headline overlay.
- Keep the panel door and directory sheet as the visual focus — this is the image's tie to
  the topic (a panel schedule actually being checked), not just generic switchgear.
- Avoid: glowing blue circuit-board abstractions, lightning bolts, handshakes, generic office
  stock, any PPE gap (bare forearms, face shield not covering the chin/neck, no rubber gloves
  under leather).

## Alt text

"An electrician in full arc-flash PPE checking a panelboard's circuit directory against the
installed breakers."

## Text warning

Image generators render lettering badly. Any writing on the directory sheet, panel labels or
breaker legends in the generated image will likely be illegible or garbled — expected and
acceptable as long as it's unreadable rather than visibly wrong text. Do not crop in on it for
any secondary use, and do not treat it as a real circuit directory.
