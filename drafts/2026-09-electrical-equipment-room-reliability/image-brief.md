# Hero image brief — How Do You Ensure Reliability in an Electrical Equipment Room?

## Generation prompt

A wide photograph of a clean, well-organized electrical equipment room in a commercial or
industrial facility, shot in 16:9 aspect ratio. A gray switchgear lineup with closed panel
doors runs along the back wall under bright, even overhead lighting. In the foreground, a
technician in correct PPE — arc-rated coveralls, a hard hat with an arc-rated face shield, and
insulated gloves — stands at a clear working distance from the panels, holding a handheld
infrared thermal-imaging camera aimed at the switchgear. The floor is bare concrete, painted
with a yellow safety boundary line in front of the equipment, with nothing stored inside it.
Cable trays and conduit run neatly overhead. The lighting is cool, bright, and slightly
clinical, like a well-maintained industrial facility rather than a moody or dramatic scene. No
readable text, signage, or labels should be prominent in the frame.

## Composition notes

- Keep the switchgear lineup and technician in the middle third of the frame so a title overlay
  can sit in the upper third without covering either.
- Leave the yellow floor boundary line visible and unobstructed — it reinforces the working
  clearance topic without needing a caption.
- Avoid clutter: no stored boxes, ladders, or loose cable on the floor. The whole point of the
  scene is a room that is doing housekeeping correctly.

## Alt text

A technician in arc-rated PPE uses an infrared camera to inspect a switchgear lineup in a
clean, well-lit electrical equipment room with clear working space marked on the floor.

## Text warning

Image generators render lettering badly. Expect any panel labels, meter displays, or safety
signage in the result to be illegible or garbled — that's expected and acceptable as long as
it reads as unreadable rather than visibly wrong. Do not crop in on any signage area.

## PPE check (human review required before this ships)

For energized switchgear work at this scale, verify the generated PPE actually matches what
C&H would specify: arc-rated coveralls or suit (not a plain coat), a full arc-rated face shield
or hood appropriate to the incident-energy category being depicted, insulated rubber gloves
with leather protectors (not plain leather gloves alone), and a hard hat. Generated images
have previously looked credible at a glance while getting the glove system or suit-vs-coat
distinction wrong — confirm before publish, per PLAYBOOK.md §6.
