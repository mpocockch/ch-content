## Composition notes

- Horizontal 16:9. Keep the open wall/ceiling space in the upper third clear of clutter — that is
  where a headline overlay would sit.
- Both workers should read as clearly separated in space (the second person visibly outside the
  work zone, not shoulder-to-shoulder with the first) — that separation is the visual point of
  this article's lead topic, the new additional-person requirement.
- Keep the switchgear cabinet and PPE in sharp focus; a shallow depth of field on the background
  conduit/wall is fine.
- Avoid: glowing blue circuit-board abstractions, lightning bolts, stock-photo handshakes, a
  generic office setting, or either worker missing a PPE layer (bare hands, no face shield, no
  hood). Incorrect PPE on an electrical safety post is a credibility problem, not a style one —
  **a human must check the rendered gear against real arc-flash PPE before this ships** (full
  hood/shield, not just safety glasses; insulating gloves plus leather protectors, not just work
  gloves; arc-rated suit, not just a hi-vis vest).

reads as a garbled, distracting attempt at real text. Do not crop in on any lettering in the
result. If review calls for a version with zero label-like marks at all, regenerate rather than
retouch.
