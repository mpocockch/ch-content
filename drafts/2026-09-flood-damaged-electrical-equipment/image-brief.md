## Composition notes

- Subject (technician + test instrument) sits right-of-center, panel and water line fill the
  left two-thirds so a headline overlay could sit in the upper-right without covering either.
- The water line and silt residue on the enclosure need to read clearly at thumbnail size. This
  is the detail that makes the topic legible at a glance. Do not crop it out.
- Keep the lockout-tagout device visible and unambiguous. Do not show the technician's hands
  inside the panel while the lockout is off.
- Avoid any visible signage, labels, or panel directory text in frame. Generated lettering
  renders badly and reviewers should not crop in expecting to read it.

## Text warning

Image generators render in-frame text and labels badly. Any lettering visible on the panel,
instrument display, or PPE in the result should be treated as decorative and unreadable, not as
real content. If any garbled text is prominent or distracting, regenerate rather than ship it.

## PPE check reminder for the reviewer (PLAYBOOK.md §6)

This scene involves an open, tagged-out (de-energized) panel and a low-voltage test instrument,
not live diagnostic work on an energized circuit, so full arc-flash PPE (suit, hood, face shield)
is not the expected gear here. Verify instead that: the disconnect reads clearly as open and
tagged, the technician is not shown reaching into the enclosure with bare hands or without the
lockout in place, and the gloves/glasses/hard hat shown are consistent with de-energized
testing and inspection work, not posed as if the panel were still live.
